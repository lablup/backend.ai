Name a permission by its bit alone: `operation` on the RBAC permission and role-preset APIs becomes `permission`, carrying `read` / `update` / `create` / `soft_delete` / `hard_delete`, and the `grant:*` operations are gone. `CreatePermissionRequest` no longer takes a scope, `object_permissions` is dropped, and the RBAC entity and operation listings answer from the wired operations rather than a hand-kept table.
