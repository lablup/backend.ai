Add `value_type: "pct"` to `user_utilization_metric`, computed by the manager as `Σcurrent / Σcapacity × 100` over the scope's kernels so clients no longer need per-metric scaling such as the cpu_util `/ 10`. The agent now reports the container and process `cpu_util` capacity as the kernel's allocated cores × 1000 instead of a constant 1000, so `cpu_util.pct` in live_stat, the Prometheus `capacity` series and the manager's pct are the share of the allocated cores (≤ 100) rather than of one core; utilization idle-checker `cpu_util` thresholds now compare against that share. The `cpu_util` unit hint is `millicores` (1000 = one core) instead of `percent`.
